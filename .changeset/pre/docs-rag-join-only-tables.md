New tutorial, "Find the right tables in a large schema": on a 63-table schema, retrieval leaves out `plans`, the model guesses a price and returns $20, and describing `plans` plus a monthly recurring revenue concept brings it back for the right $38. Its example is `examples/find-the-right-tables`. "RAG for large schemas" gets a "Check that retrieval finds every table" step with the same case, and the troubleshooting entry, Enrich your schema, How AskDB works, The schema artifact and the schema artifact format now say that retrieval matches enrichment and that a retrieved concept brings every table it links.
