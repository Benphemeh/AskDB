Enrich your schema has a worked example: it extends the Quickstart's `shop.db` with `plans` and `subscriptions` tables, asks for the monthly recurring revenue, and shows the SQL the model writes before and after you describe the `status` codes and the rule for an active subscription, in Studio or in `tables/subscriptions.md` (#955). The guide also links the askdb-enrich-schema agent skill.
